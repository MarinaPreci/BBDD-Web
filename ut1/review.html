UT1 Review — Classroom Activities

01

Spot the Problem

👥 groups of 3🗣 group discussion + short oral report
Instructions: Read your group's case carefully. Discuss it together and answer the four questions in English. Choose one reporter to explain your answer to the class in about one minute.
CASE A

The Electronics Shop

An online electronics shop has two departments. The Warehouse team keeps a spreadsheet with each customer's shipping address, to send out orders. The Customer Service team keeps a different spreadsheet with the same customers' contact details, to answer complaints. Last week, a customer called Warehouse to update her address. Two days later, Customer Service sent her an email confirming a delivery — to her old address.

CASE B

The Language Academy

A small language academy keeps student records in two separate systems: one for attendance (managed by the teachers) and one for payments (managed by the office). Both systems use the student's full name as the only identifier — there is no shared ID number. When two students happen to have the same name, the office cannot always tell which payment record belongs to which attendance record.

CASE C

The Gym Front Desk

A gym has one shared computer at the front desk. Two receptionists are both updating the same membership file at the same time: one is adding a new member, the other is renewing an existing membership. When both save their changes, one of the two updates disappears completely.

Questions — answer for your case

  1. What exactly goes wrong in your case? Describe it in your own words.
  2. Which file problem(s) does this illustrate — data redundancy, inconsistency, data isolation, or concurrent access? More than one may apply — or none, if you think it's something else.
  3. How would storing this data in a proper database instead of separate files solve the problem?
  4. Prepare a one-minute explanation for the class.
02

Design Your Table

👥 groups of 2🗣 written design + peer review
Instructions: Your group has been given a real-world situation. Design one table that could store this information in a database. In the last five minutes, you will swap your worksheet with another group and review each other's work.
GROUP 1

School Library

The library needs to keep track of every book it owns.

GROUP 2

Gym

The gym needs to keep track of every member.

GROUP 3

Veterinary Clinic

The clinic needs to keep track of every animal patient.

GROUP 4

Car Rental Company

The company needs to keep track of every vehicle in its fleet.

Your task

  1. List at least 4 fields your table needs, with a short description of what each one stores.
  2. Write one example record — real-looking, made-up data for all 4 fields.
  3. Choose your primary key and justify it: why is this field unique for every record? Why couldn't you use any of the other fields instead?
  4. Decide: would an index be useful for this table? Explain briefly.
